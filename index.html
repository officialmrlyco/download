What is new in v1.3.8

  • A redesigned Offers catalogue with compact routes, fixed search and category controls, safer collision review, and clearer mapping, verifier, retry, and Auto Time actions.
  • A guided Relay setup for Sender and Receiver using SMS, Online, Hotspot/Wi-Fi, or Bluetooth, with built-in QR pairing and normal transaction history.
  • More reliable payment queues, pending-work recovery, retry wake-ups, and accurate Accessibility readiness so one failed handoff cannot hold every later transaction.
  • Refreshed Settings, Site Link, Auto Reply, Client, Transaction Detail, Quick Dial, and Statistics screens with safer editing and clearer save or failure feedback.
  • Quick Dial contact search keeps customer-number input numeric, while Auto Reply templates use protected bottom-sheet editing.
  • Stronger security PIN recovery, mapping-learning validation, Relay duplicate protection, sender auto-reply isolation, and lower-memory transaction and M-Pesa history handling.
